A cinematic commercial voice — calm, composed, quietly powerful. Premium brand-reveal feel: short sentences, deliberate pauses, warm but restrained empathy. Plain English, no technical jargon. Build slowly from the problem to the promise.

Think back to the last time a typhoon hit our town. The barangays knew what was happening — but the record did not exist. One report arrived by SMS. Another was written on paper. A third was carried over the radio. Each one true. Each one scattered. A town speaking, but no single voice being heard. And when reports are scattered, the Municipal office cannot show a complete, solid record of what happened, where, and to whom. And without that record, a State of Calamity cannot be declared — and every day that decision waits is a day help waits too.

So we built one dependable record. Saster gathers every report from every barangay into a single, living record — always there, always ready, always complete. And we built it for the worst of days. When the power is out. When the internet is gone. Your report is still saved on your device — and it arrives the moment your connection returns. Nothing is lost. Nothing depends on one phone call, or one slip of paper. So when the time comes, our officials can declare a State of Calamity as soon as possible — with complete, reliable evidence behind it.

Now let me show you how it works. Begin by signing in. Your username and password are given to you by your administrator — the chairman or the municipal office. Type them in, and you'll land on your dashboard: a summary of alerts, recent reports, and the tools you need for the day.

The heart of Saster is your incident report. Tap Create Incident, and a simple form guides you. First, choose the Disaster Type — here, we're reporting a flood. Then tap the map to drop a pin at the exact location, or type the nearest landmark. Responders don't guess. They go straight to the spot.

Next comes the impact. Affected — three. Injured — five. Dead — none. Missing — two. Type the numbers you know; leave them at zero where you don't have a figure yet. Tell us whether evacuation is needed, and if the road leading to the area is open or blocked — that one detail can change everything for a rescue team.

Use the Notes field for what the numbers can't say. Road blocked near sitio. A short sentence can save a team precious time.

And a picture is worth a thousand words. Attach up to five photos or two short videos right from your phone or camera — evidence that travels with the report, so decision-makers see it as clearly as you do. No signal? No problem. Saster saves your report on your device and sends it the moment your connection returns. You'll find these in Saved Reports, safe and waiting for retry.

Tap Submit, and the report is on its way. Your chairman reviews it, then forwards it to the Municipal office for action — and if a wide-area warning is needed, an alert goes out to every barangay in seconds. The same report can also reach the Provincial Health Office, so medical needs and outbreaks are never missed.

From your first login to the moment help arrives, Saster keeps one clear picture of what's happening across the whole municipality. Every report you file becomes part of the record that moves your town toward help and recovery. You've done your part — now the responders have the facts, and help is on the way.

Simple to report, certain to reach, ready even offline.
